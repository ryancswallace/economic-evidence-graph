# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in the Economic Evidence Graph community a
respectful and harassment-free experience.

## Expected Behavior

* Use welcoming and inclusive language.
* Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
* Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
* Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, sustained disruption,
personal attacks, or publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report concerns to Ryan Wallace at <ryancswallace@gmail.com>. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as possible.
